What is a thou?

One thou is one thousandth of an inch: 0.001 in. It is machinist shorthand for talking about tiny movements and tolerances without reciting a pile of zeros. Odd sixteenths land between two whole thou, so this trainer accepts either nearest reading.

10 thou = 0.010 in 62.5 thou = 1/16 in 250 thou = 1/4 in

What is a tenth? In machining, one tenth means one ten-thousandth of an inch: 0.0001 in. It is one-tenth of one thou, so 10 tenths equal 1 thou.

How small is that?

Kitchen foil: about 0.5–1 thou

Human hair: about 2–5 thou across

Printer paper: about 4 thou thick
