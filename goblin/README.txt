GOBLIN SHOP — PRIVATE REVIEW BUILD
==================================

Goblin Shop is the next creative branch of TurdGobbler's Arena deck-name
generator. It preserves the current Ultimate compiler and layer engine while
turning the 64-character limit into a shop economy.

REVIEW
------

Open index.html in a modern browser.

1. Type a deck name in YOUR ORDER.
2. Watch the CHARACTER COINS purse subtract text, dye, and charm costs.
3. Drag DYE, CHARMS, or SPRITES from the shelves to the riverbank.
4. Click CAULDRON and choose a supported charm to affect the entire name.
5. Click a purchased cauldron charm to tune or refund it.
6. Drag placed riverbank wares to reposition them or return them for a refund.
7. Click the finished name in the clearwater stream to copy it for Arena.

SHOP RULES
----------

- The purse contains exactly 64 raw Arena characters.
- Typed letters are paid for first.
- Every colour tag, effect tag, and sprite has its exact raw-code price.
- A purchase that would exceed 64 is refused and reports the shortfall.
- Explicit effects are preserved; automatic gradient stages yield first.
- The clear black rock/shadow beneath the stream keeps the Arena comparison
  surface accurate while the surrounding water provides the shop fantasy.

PRESERVED ULTIMATE FEATURES
---------------------------

- One shared rail for colour, FX, and sprites
- Exact letter-versus-sprite effect targeting
- WUBRG staged sessions and named identities
- Looks, four saved recipes, recent colours, palettes, rotate, and flip
- Whole-name All Caps and Small Caps
- Undo, Redo, Start Over, Clear FX, local persistence, and keyboard controls
- Bundled Arena font and all sixteen sprite assets
- Crunchy haptics with safe fallback on unsupported devices
- Offline operation, reduced motion, and screen-reader status updates

Goblin Shop uses its own browser-storage key. On first visit it can inherit the
current Ultimate design, but subsequent Goblin Shop edits do not overwrite the
Ultimate save.

This build is not published. /colour and /ultimate remain unchanged.
