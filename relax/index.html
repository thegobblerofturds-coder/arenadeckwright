Tap a large bubble to split it into a chain of little bubbles. Sweep through the little bubbles to pop them. Drag gently to stretch; a hard pull bursts the bubble. Bubble flow increases as you pop. Sound starts with your first tap or keypress. Use your device volume to adjust it. With a keyboard, use the arrow keys to choose a bubble and press Space or Enter to burst it.
